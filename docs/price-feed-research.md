# Which price feeds should Price Verse launch first?

US product research · November 20, 2026

**Start with Harris Teeter and Food Lion for repeat use, Booking.com for a high-dollar demonstration, and Best Buy as a check on comparison quality.** A grocery shopper can bring back a second observation next week. Two travelers can reproduce a hotel search before either books. A Best Buy SKU gives the team a quick way to catch a false match. Launch a broad reporting directory, but recruit contributors around a few fixed offers. Forty-five brands with one quote apiece would give a shopper zero within-brand comparisons.

## What Uber’s restriction covers

The warning on Uber’s fare-estimate endpoint refers specifically to comparisons with competing services. Two riders looking at UberX from the same pickup to the same destination are asking a within-Uber question. The endpoint still requires Uber approval. Its documented output is a product-level estimate that can be a range. Even with a key, that range cannot establish the exact upfront fare on either rider’s screen. [Uber fare estimates](https://developer.uber.com/docs/riders/references/api/v1.2/estimates-price-get)

A separate provision in section III.G of the Rider API terms addresses aggregated displays with competitors, parsing or bulk collection of pricing data, and sharing API-derived data through a service that also features a competitor. Price Verse includes Lyft elsewhere. A separate Uber chart answers the statistical question, but the broader platform language still matters for API integration. My recommendation is to retain within-Uber comparisons and ask Uber specifically about rider-authorized upfront quotes, the presence of Lyft elsewhere in the directory, public aggregate display and retention. A manually reported price, a browser-assisted observation and an API-derived price have different acquisition paths. The API terms alone do not settle every path. Uber’s business receipts service covers designated business trips for eligible enterprise clients. [Rider API terms](https://developer.uber.com/docs/riders/terms-of-use) · [US consumer terms](https://www.uber.com/us/en/legal/general-terms-of-use/) · [Business receipts](https://developer.uber.com/docs/businesses/receipts/introduction)

## Launch ranking

These scores are product judgments for an early US launch. Frequency measures opportunities for repeat contributions. Stakes measure the value of a decision that a comparison could change. Matching measures how reliably independent shoppers can identify the same offer. Access measures how readily shoppers can contribute or a useful reference can be obtained. Each runs from 1 to 5. The total gives frequency and stakes 25% each, matching 30%, and access 20%. A high score does not imply access to a signed-in account API.

| Rank | Sector | Frequency | Stakes | Matching | Access | /100 | First experiment |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | Groceries and household staples | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 91 | Recruit around two Charlotte stores and ten exact packaged items. |
| 2 | Hotels | 3 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 80 | Repeat the same refundable room search on desktop and mobile. |
| 3 | Consumer tech and electronics | 3 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 76 | Compare one new, first-party Best Buy SKU across membership conditions. |
| 4 | Home broadband | 2 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 71 | Compare the same local plan and price stage across households, with a published plan label as a reference. |
| 5 | Airlines | 3 | 5 | 3 | 2 | 66 | Coordinate simultaneous quotes for one CLT departure, fare family and bag allowance. |
| 6 | General retail and marketplaces | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 65 | Start with first-party inventory. Audit every apparent gap for a seller, shipping or condition change. |
| 7 | Software and subscriptions | 2 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 61 | Compare renewal offers for the same Adobe plan, term and billing channel. |
| 8 | Meal delivery | 4 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 59 | Rebuild one restaurant order, including modifiers, time and fees, before comparing totals. |
| 9 | Rides | 4 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 50 | Have two riders request an estimate concurrently at identical public landmarks. |

Hotels win on potential dollars per booking. Electronics wins on ease of matching. If the first objective is to validate the software’s matching accuracy, run Best Buy before hotels. If the first objective is a result a shopper can act on during checkout, run the hotel experiment first.

## Evidence behind the shortlist

**Groceries and staples.** US consumer units spent an average of $6,224 on food at home in 2024, plus $877 on housekeeping supplies. Packaged coffee, detergent and diapers have repeat demand and identifiable sizes. In a coordinated Consumer Reports study, the same 18-ounce cereal at one Washington, DC Safeway was offered on Instacart for $2.99, $3.49 and $3.69. Instacart announced the end of item price testing on December 22, 2025. The study demonstrates a way to reproduce an observation. It cannot establish that those experiments continue. For a Charlotte pilot, Harris Teeter’s digital coupon deductions appear on qualifying receipt lines, and Food Lion offers personalized monthly MVP offers. Capture shelf or app price, clipped discount and redemption conditions separately when auditing a gap. A reward for a future trip belongs outside today’s item price. [BLS spending table](https://www.bls.gov/opub/reports/consumer-expenditures/2024/home.htm) · [Study data and methodology](https://github.com/consumerreports/instacart) · [Instacart announcement](https://company.instacart.com/updates/ending-item-price-tests-on-instacart) · [Harris Teeter](https://www.harristeeter.com/i/hc/help/faqs/mobile/digital-coupons) · [Food Lion](https://foodlion.com/rewards)

**Hotels.** Booking.com explicitly documents platform, booker country, stay dates, authentication and partner configuration as inputs to rate eligibility. Marriott documents a preferred member rate on its direct channels. These give Price Verse something unusually valuable to tell a traveler: a lower quote may be reproducible by signing in or using mobile, with the same cancellation rights. Compare the entire stay in one currency including mandatory charges and tax, and check for amounts payable at the property. Run Booking.com observations together and Marriott-direct observations together, even for the same hotel. [Booking.com rate eligibility](https://developers.booking.com/demand/docs/accommodations/discounts-target) · [Marriott member rates](https://www.marriott.com/online-hotel-booking.mi) · [Expedia fee presentation](https://developers.expediagroup.com/rapid/setup/launch-requirements/lodging-launch-reqs) · [FTC lodging and ticket fees](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/rule-unfair-or-deceptive-fees-frequently-asked-questions)

**Tech and general retail.** Best Buy documents exclusive member pricing as well as a catalog API. Start with a single new item sold by Best Buy. Use the exact SKU, configuration, bundle and fulfillment method. Show the membership requirement beside a lower price so someone can judge the economics. Amazon is attractive for breadth, but an unchanged product page can feature a different merchant. Costco explicitly distinguishes warehouse promotions and online shipping costs. Grouping by product title alone can produce a convincing and entirely misleading spread. [Best Buy membership](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/electronics/best-buy-membership/pcmcat1679668833285.c?id=pcmcat1679668833285) · [Best Buy developer catalog](https://developer.bestbuy.com/apis) · [Amazon merchant offers](https://assets.aboutamazon.com/d6/09/381147c54c478a7917faee4b2059/amazon-dma-public-compliance-report.pdf) · [Costco price differences](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/691)

**Broadband and subscriptions.** A persistent difference of $15 a month would be $180 a year. That is an illustration, not measured savings. Check the full promotion duration, upload and download speeds, equipment, autopay, bundle and renewal stage. Xfinity provides an address-specific label lookup. A ZIP is useful for discovery, but it does not guarantee the same service at neighboring buildings. The FCC’s 2026 order moved to eliminate the separate machine-readable file requirement, so verify individual provider downloads before committing to ongoing coverage. For software, Adobe’s published table distinguishes contract and renewal conditions. A plan billed monthly can still carry an annual commitment. [Xfinity broadband labels](https://www.xfinity.com/broadband-labels) · [2026 FCC order and effective-date provisions](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-08-13/pdf/2026-16503.pdf) · [Adobe plan changes](https://helpx.adobe.com/account/individual/subscriptions-and-plans/plan-types-and-eligibility/changes-to-individual-plan.html)

**Airlines.** The Bureau of Transportation Statistics reports a $387 average domestic itinerary fare in 2025. That describes purchased itineraries across markets. A live comparison needs specific flights and terms. Duffel documents that offers expire, typically within 30 minutes, with the exact expiry supplied per offer. Concurrent observations matter even within a 15-minute display window. Hold the date, direction, marketing flight number, passenger count, cabin, fare brand, payment currency, storefront and baggage constant. A fare disappearing from inventory while the second shopper searches is a plausible explanation for a gap. [BTS ticket-fare methodology](https://www.bts.gov/newsroom/2025-annual-average-domestic-air-fare-decreases-2024) · [Duffel offers](https://duffel.com/docs/api/v2/offers/price-offer) · [Fare conditions](https://duffel.com/docs/guides/displaying-offer-and-order-conditions)

**Rides and delivery.** Both are compelling to demonstrate and expensive to match reliably. Rides need the same pickup entrance, destination entrance, service tier and request moment. Two ZIP pairs can conceal very different driving distances. Delivery needs the same restaurant branch, basket, modifiers, delivery area, membership, service level and checkout stage. DoorDash documents that DashPass savings depend on eligibility and subtotal minimums. Two otherwise similar orders can cross that threshold. [Uber estimate inputs](https://developer.uber.com/docs/riders/references/api/v1.2/estimates-price-get) · [DashPass eligibility](https://help.doordash.com/consumers/s/article/What-is-DashPass)

## Pilot design and decision rules

1. **Charlotte groceries.** Pick one branch per retailer and ten shelf-stable products sold in exact pack sizes. Invite shoppers to reopen a shared comparison so branch names and product details carry over. Ask for a first quote without clipping a new coupon, then record any reproducible coupon explanation separately. Repeat across three shopping sessions. One shopper contributes one current observation per matched offer.
2. **Hotels.** Select three properties on Booking.com, two future date pairs per property, one room, two adults and the same cancellation and meal terms. Have pairs check within two minutes. First repeat under identical channel and eligibility conditions, then change one factor, such as mobile versus desktop. Record the full-stay price at the same checkout stage. A second independent shopper should be able to reproduce any advertised saving.
3. **Best Buy.** Choose five readily identifiable SKUs. Compare two shoppers with identical eligibility, then a member and a nonmember. Record a zero gap as a valid outcome. Manually review open-box condition, a changed seller and bundles before accepting an apparent spread.
4. **Decide using matched coverage.** Track the fraction of requested comparisons with at least two distinct shoppers, median time to a second quote, weekly returning contributors, and the proportion of reviewed gaps that survive the offer audit. As an initial decision rule, aim for a second observation on half the seeded offers and at least 90% genuinely comparable pairs in manual review. These are proposed targets. Record results even when every matched quote agrees.

For public reporting, show the number of independent shoppers, observation times, and known eligibility differences. A spread demonstrates observed prices. Attributing the spread to a characteristic or algorithm requires controlled replication. The FTC’s surveillance-pricing research describes intermediary capabilities and uses hypothetical examples to protect confidential information. It does not establish what any particular brand in this directory does. [FTC research summary](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/01/ftc-surveillance-pricing-study-indicates-wide-range-personal-data-used-set-individualized-consumer)

## Scope of this build

The directory includes **45 shopper-reporting providers across nine sectors**. It supports direct reports, a paired browser companion, and compatible apps posting to a Price Verse feed. Official merchant account connections and reference API imports are research opportunities. No merchant credentials or live customer accounts were used in this investigation.

Travel prices are USD cash quotes. The flight form covers one adult on a nonstop one-way itinerary. The hotel form covers one room with adults and has separate stay dates, occupancy and storefront country. Groceries and electronics use product IDs alongside variant details. Subscriptions distinguish billing terms and price stage. Broadband uses service ZIP and price stage. Text-entered conditions can still fragment matches. Time windows are fixed buckets, so quotes on opposite sides of a boundary can land in different comparisons even when only seconds apart.
